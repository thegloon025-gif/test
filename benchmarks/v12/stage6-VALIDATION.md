## Diagnosis from Stage 5, before discovery

The 20 previously recorded Zhulodok no-follow-up cases divide into **10 without a qualifying hand spell, seven T8 horizon cases and three actual mana-payment shortfalls**. Stage 5's original fresh B3 holdout had no Zhulodok deployment; B4 deployed it in one seed per pilot, without a qualifying follow-up. Its fixed follow-up policy changed none of those measured vectors. The evidence implicates access, late deployment and resources; it does not justify simply boosting cascade tags or blaming post-action exhausted mana.

Realized supported draw was sparse: under conservative decisions, B3's 16 old holdout runs produced 3/6/2 total effect draws across commander-first/development/native; B4 produced 9/10/9. Those are actual prefix receipts, not proof that unsupported real draw engines never work. Native execution already consumes substantial resources: B3 paid 29/24/24 copying triggers, spending 58/48/48 true-C units in those cohorts; B4 paid 24/21/24, spending 48/42/48. Copy triggers provide actual value but are not free mana development. Stage 6 adds packages intended to make available follow-ups and access usable while keeping the native engines and damage outlets.

## Integration decision and manual next step

**Evidence is insufficient to justify a separately reviewed opt-in gameplay-aware construction trial yet.** The obstacle is uncertain/incomplete complete-deck evidence, not failure to generate safe alternatives or a requirement to spend more. No candidate earns an improved recommendation; production selection must stay unchanged.

The smallest useful next improvement is a **prospectively fixed, exact tutor-target/follow-up planning policy**: choose among real legal tutor targets using actual supported future deployment/payment prerequisites, keep default and planned policies separate, and test whether the observed seven-MV availability bottleneck changes. Couple this with one bounded lower-setup access package frontier (e.g. the already discovered supported cast-draw candidates) rather than simply adding more expensive draw engines. Re-register samples/criteria before evaluating it; do not tune on this holdout. A complete rules simulator or more static weights are not prerequisites.
