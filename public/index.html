Research details and manual overrides (optional)

Ranked discovery checks public Archidekt lists against Commander Spellbook. Saved bracket-matched evidence is available to Architect. No saved deck is required for the commander query. This panel is optional for troubleshooting or manually adding a specific deck. Import a public Archidekt deck by URL. CommanderOracle stores the cardlist locally, verifies it against Scryfall, and measures which cards appear together. v1.1 identifies recurring packages and distinct deck archetypes with source attribution. When enough eligible bracket-matched lists exist, strategy-first construction can adapt entire verified source structures (spells and lands) alongside independent local archetypes. A local-only baseline is always available, and observed popularity is not proof of strength. Recorded, on-demand Spellbook variants remain an independent local combo source.
